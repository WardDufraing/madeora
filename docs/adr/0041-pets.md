# ADR 0041: Pets, medical history, medical access and care

- **Status:** Accepted for S31 within the approved architecture (§4, §6, §9, §15, §19, §21, §34, §36, §39, §43), CLAUDE.md (authorization C, database access A, file storage B, forms A, internationalization A, date/time A, €0 budget), ADR 0011 and 0014 (permission engine, delegable capabilities), ADR 0019 and 0020 (file storage, trash) and ADR 0025 (task attachments). The defaults in the brief (`plans/steps/S31-brief.md`, "Defaults picked") are open to correction by the owner. Ward kept S31 as one step with care schedules (2026-11-04) and approved the permission matrix.
- **Date:** 2026-11-04
- **Step:** S31 (brief: `plans/steps/S31-brief.md`).

## Context

The spec (Modules.md §4.6 and its confirmed decisions) gives each Home pet profiles with mostly optional fields, a structured medical history, and a separate permission for medical information that is not the same as being able to manage pets. Medical information must be hidden from members without that permission. The done-condition for this step is that the "Pet profile and medical history" journey passes and medical information is hidden from members without permission, proven by RLS and interface tests.

## Decision

**Two independent permissions.** Pet access (`private.pet_access`, mirrored by `decidePetAction` in `packages/domain/src/permissions/pets.ts`) is _manage_ for the Owner and for Administrators with the new delegable capability `manageHomePets`, and _view_ for every other member. Medical access (`private.pet_medical_level`) is a per-member, per-Home level in `private.pet_medical_access`: _view_ or _manage_; no row means none. The Owner is always _manage_. A level is set by the Owner or by an Administrator with the new capability `managePetMedicalAccess`, never for themselves and never for the Owner (`canChangeMedicalAccess`). The grant row references the membership and is deleted with it, so leaving or removal resets to none, and rejoining starts at none. After an ownership transfer the new Owner is _manage_ implicitly; the former Owner keeps only an explicit grant, if any. Every change is audited (`pet.medical_access.changed`, ids and levels only) and takes effect on the next request: every read, file authorization and mutation rechecks it.

**Sensitive data in its own tables.** The basic profile (`private.pets`) holds everything every member sees: name, species or a custom label, breed, a birth date _or_ an approximate age with the date it was recorded (never turned into a birthday), sex, neutered, weight with unit and date, veterinarian contact, diet, care instructions, notes and the photo. Allergies, medications, identification numbers and notes, and medical notes are in `private.pet_medical_details`; the history in `private.pet_medical_entries`; supporting documents in `private.pet_medical_attachments`. None of these tables is granted to an API role; every read and write is a security-definer function.

**Unknown, None and empty.** Fields that may be explicitly Unknown are listed per row (`unknown_fields`), and allergies and medications may be recorded as None (`none_fields`). A field with a value cannot also be Unknown or None; empty means not entered and is never shown as None.

**Leak-proof concealment.** Without medical view, `get_pet_medical`, `get_pet_medical_entry` and every medical mutation answer exactly like a pet or entry that does not exist (`null` / `not_found`), also for a forged id. The pet list and profile carry no count or hint of medical records, and the page does not even read them. File reads of attachments go through `authorize_file_read`, which checks medical view at that moment; download links last 60 seconds. Medical entry pages have a neutral document title.

**Medical history.** An entry has a type (vet visit, vaccination, treatment, test or diagnosis, surgery, other) and an event date between the pet's birth date (or 1980-01-01) and two years after the Home's today; title, veterinarian, location, treatment, notes and a follow-up on or after the event are optional. Entries after the Home's today are shown as Upcoming appointments, apart from the history. Edits are corrections in place with a revision check (`stale`). No reminders, regimes or expiry dates are derived from entries (spec); recurring care is the separate care schedule below.

**Files.** The S15 pipeline with two purposes: `pet_photo` (JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 10 MB, re-encoded as WebP without metadata, made the pet's photo on finalize) and `pet_medical_attachment` (JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF up to 10 MB, checked by the task-attachment checker, 10 per entry counting uploads in progress). Both count toward the Home's storage pool and recheck the person's rights on finalize; nothing is attached before finalize answers.

**Trash.** Deleting a pet is one 7-day trash entry that takes its photo, details, entries and attachments with it (the attachments' files are held in `private.pet_trash_files`, not the shared `trash_entry_files`, so the entry's file count never reveals medical documents); only people with pet manage **and** medical _manage_ see, restore or purge it early (Ward, 2026-11-04; for every pet, so the rule never reveals whether a pet has medical records), and restoring needs room under the pet limit. Deleting an entry or removing an attachment is its own trash entry, visible and restorable only for people with medical _manage_ at that moment, and refused while its pet (or, for an attachment, its entry) is in the trash. Purging a pet or an entry purges its open child entries. Audit events carry ids and levels only, never names of medicines, titles or notes.

**Care schedules** (`20261117090000_pet_care.sql`). An activity (`private.pet_care_activities`) has a title, a category (feeding, medication, grooming, vet appointment, other), optional instructions, a "contains medical details" flag that is always on for medication, a reminder offset (off, 0, 15, 60 or 1440 minutes) and a state (active, paused, ended). Its schedule is a chain of parts (`private.pet_care_parts`, the chore part pattern): once, or the S19 recurrence from a start with an optional end, an optional Home-local time and 0–10 responsible members (none is Unassigned). Occurrences (`private.pet_care_occurrences`, unique per activity and date) are created up to 7 days ahead by reads and the hourly job, never late outside a 7-day overdue window; status is derived (Upcoming, Due, Overdue, Completed, Skipped, Paused). Changing the schedule "from this occurrence on" supersedes the current part from that date; changing one occurrence detaches it, and later schedule changes leave it alone. Pause and end remove untouched open occurrences from today on; resume continues from today. Every completion, skip, reopen and correction is kept in `private.pet_care_events`.

**Care permissions and concealment.** Every member sees care and completes it; pet managers add, change, pause, resume, end, delete and restore activities and change, skip, reopen or correct any occurrence; a responsible member may skip, and whoever recorded a completion may reopen or correct it. An activity with medical details needs medical _manage_ as well to add, change or delete. For anyone without medical view at that moment such an activity shows only its category: never its title, instructions or completion notes, in pages, the widget, notification names, the trash or errors; correcting its note is refused to them.

**Care reminders, widget and vacations.** A reminder is an outbox job (`pet-care-reminder`, keyed by occurrence and moment) at the occurrence time (08:00 Home time without one) minus the offset; delivery rechecks the occurrence, the activity and each responsible member, and notifies them (`pet_care.reminder`, category reminders, ids-only parameters; the in-app text names the care only for readers who may see it, and the email never does). Unassigned care is not reminded. The `pet-care` widget (Home dashboards and Home-sourced on the personal one) lists open care from the overdue window up to 1, 3 or 7 days for one pet or all, everyone's, the viewer's or unassigned, with Done. The vacation reminder adds how many active care activities have dates during the vacation and how many are unassigned; vacations never pause care.

**Limits.** 50 pets per Home, 1000 entries per pet, 10 attachments per entry, 100 care activities per pet; text limits are in `private.pet_limit` and `PET_LIMITS` in `apps/web/src/pets/schema.ts`.

**Interface and API.** Pets is in the Home navigation (`/homes/[homeId]/pets`): cards with photo, name, species and age, and Add pet (name only). The pet page shows the basic sections to every member and a Medical section (sensitive details, Upcoming appointments, History) only to medical viewers, with edit controls only for managers. Entry pages (`/homes/[homeId]/pets/[petId]/medical/[entryId]`) show the details and documents. The member page has a Pet medical information card (No access · View · View and edit) for whoever may grant it. Routes: `GET/POST /api/v1/pets`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/pets/:id`, `GET/PUT /api/v1/pets/:id/medical`, `POST /api/v1/pets/:id/medical-entries`, `POST/DELETE /api/v1/pets/:id/photo`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/pet-medical-entries/:id`, `POST /api/v1/pet-medical-entries/:id/attachments`, `DELETE /api/v1/pet-medical-attachments/:id`, `GET/PUT /api/v1/homes/:homeId/pet-medical-access`. Care: the pet page has a Care section, and each activity its page (`/homes/[homeId]/pets/[petId]/care/[activityId]`, neutral document title); routes `GET/POST /api/v1/pets/:id/care`, `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/pet-care/:id`, `POST /api/v1/pet-care/:id/state`, `POST /api/v1/pet-care-occurrences/:id`, `GET /api/v1/pet-care?homeId=`.

## Consequences

- Any new read of medical data (a widget, notifications, Household Display, export, search, Ora) must apply the same rule: nothing without medical view at that moment, and no answer that differs from a missing record. The care pages, widget and reminders follow it for activities with medical details.
- Any new way a membership ends must keep deleting its grant (the foreign key does this today).
- Known limits, accepted for this step: medical access is per Home, not per pet or field (excluded by the spec); no reminders from follow-up dates; care is never completed by a reminder or a schedule, and missed care never escalates; no Photos module integration (S35).
- A personal export (S38) and account deletion (S40) must include pets, medical data and grants according to their own rules.
- **Rollback and compatibility:** the migration adds tables, functions, two delegable capabilities, a trash module with three item types and two upload purposes. It recreates `authorize_file_read` and wraps `expire_upload` and the trash dispatch functions (old versions renamed `*_before_pets` in `private`). It changes no existing data. The care migration adds its own tables and functions, a trash item type, a notification kind and job, and wraps the trash dispatch functions again (`*_before_pet_care`) and recreates `deliver_vacation_reminder`. Both are forward-only; repairing them on production means a new migration. Rolling the app back to S30c code while they stay applied leaves pets and care unreachable in the interface; queued care reminders then fail as an unknown job kind, with no other effect.
