# ADR 0028: Chore proposals, volunteer requests, the overview, the calendar and reminders

- **Status:** Accepted for S21 within the approved architecture (§8 Realtime C, §9, §11, §39), CLAUDE.md (database access A, authorization C, data fetching B, background processing B, forms A, date/time A, frontend state A, dashboard layout B, €0 budget) and the spec's sections on proposals, reminders, the manager digest, the overview layouts and the chore calendar (PS L1579–L1718) and on volunteer requests (PS L1963–L2031). The defaults below are open to correction by the owner.
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
- **Step:** S21, its second and third parts (brief: `plans/steps/S21-brief.md`, "S21b" and "S21c"). Builds on ADR 0027 (pool and claims), ADR 0026 (chores), ADR 0024 (task reminders) and ADR 0025 (task calendar and widgets).

## Context

Members want to suggest chores without managing them. A chore that lost its assignee needs someone, and a manager wants to ask a few people rather than wait for the pool. The Home needs an at-a-glance week of who does what, a calendar where a chore manager moves one occurrence, a personal reminder for the person doing a chore, and a short daily summary for managers of what needs them.

## Decision

**Two more migrations, no exposed tables.** `20261102090000_chore_proposals_volunteers.sql` (proposals, volunteer requests and invitations) and `20261103090000_chore_overview_reminders.sql` (reminders, the schedule read, the move impact, the summary). Every read and change is a `security definer` function with the person's session; the jobs' functions are for `service_role` only.

**Proposals.** Every current member proposes a title, instructions, category, estimate, checklist and due day; chore managers review. Pending → Approved, Rejected (reason 1–500) or Withdrawn by the proposer. Approval may revise every field, shows the final values and is confirmed; it creates one unassigned one-off chore (claim mode All, expiry Overdue) in the same transaction, under the proposal's lock and at the revision the reviewer saw, so two reviewers cannot publish twice. A person cannot have two pending proposals with the same title; reviewers see "Possible duplicate" when another pending proposal or an open chore has it. The proposer gets an in-app notice of the decision (category `chores`). No drafts, no points (S23).

**Volunteer requests.** For an open occurrence that needs reassignment, a chore manager invites 1–20 current members; one open request per occurrence. While it is open the occurrence shows "Awaiting volunteer" and is not claimable. The first valid acceptance wins under the chore lock and becomes the only assignee; other pending invitations close. Decline needs no reason. The manager withdraws one invitation, adds members not invited before, or cancels. The request expires at the end of the due day in the Home's timezone (default picked), lazily, and an acceptance at or after the deadline is refused even before that; it is never reopened. Invitees see the chore and their own invitation only, never the roster. A manager can correct an accepted volunteer before submission, with a reason and a preview (event `assignment_corrected`).

**The schedule read.** `list_chore_schedule(home, from, to)` answers at most 42 days and 600 items: each occurrence with its state in words-ready flags, and the dates of recurring chores still to be created, marked `projected` (shown as Planned, never movable). The Chores calendar and the widget both use it.

**Chores calendar.** `/homes/:id/chores/calendar`, week or month, filters by member (or Unassigned) and category, a day panel listing that day's chores. The address holds the whole view, so it reads without JavaScript. Chore managers move one existing open occurrence that is not pending approval or in rework, by dragging it onto a day (a pointer shortcut that opens the Move dialog on that day) or with its **Move** button (keyboard and touch). The dialog previews old and new date and assignee and the effects, then confirms at the revision seen. Moving reuses `update_chore_occurrence` with `dryRun`; its impact gains `overdue`, `remindersMoved`, `remindersEnded`, `handoverEnded`, `volunteerEnded` and `releaseNow` (a kept claim whose automatic release moment the new due date puts in the past). A change with `releaseNow` is made only with `confirmRelease`; otherwise the answer is `409 needs-confirmation`. Any due-date change of a claimed occurrence (single, bulk or series) queues its automatic release again for the new deadline. Only that occurrence changes. The main Calendar's chore overlay comes with the Calendar module.

**Personal chore reminders.** Like task reminders (ADR 0024): an assignee sets one reminder per occurrence, at a date and time or 0–30 days before the due day at a chosen time, in the Home's timezone; the database previews the moment (DST gap and overlap included) and refuses a moment already past. The outbox job `chore-reminder` (keyed by occurrence, person and revision) delivers through `deliver_chore_reminder`, which re-checks everything (category `reminders`). A reminder ends when the person stops being an assignee and when the occurrence closes; a days-before reminder follows a changed due date, and one whose new moment has passed ends. Nobody sees another person's reminder.

**Daily summary (default picked: off, in the manager's own timezone).** A chore manager turns it on per Home and picks an hour. The hourly schedule `chore-digest` sends at most one `chores` notice per Home, manager and local day, only when something qualifies: overdue with or without an assignee, waiting for approval, in rework, needing reassignment, or a claim returning to the pool within 24 hours. The notice carries counts; its link opens the live **Daily summary** page, which checks the reader's rights when it opens. Email follows the person's `chores` preference. It never changes chores.

**Chore schedule widget.** A Home widget (on its dashboard, or bound to a Home on the personal one): the Home-local week with the Home's week start, as a member-by-day matrix or daily cards grouped by member, Week or Day, previous, next and Today. A cell lists up to its limit (2, 3 or 5; default 3) and "+N more" opens the rest in place. Below, the unassigned chores of the shown week from the pool with **Claim**; in Day view they are filtered to the day with a link to the whole week. States are always words. Settings hold the default layout and the limit; a viewer's own layout switch is remembered in their browser per widget (`localStorage`, read after hydration; default picked). Below about 42rem of widget width the matrix becomes a list of the selected day. The current week comes with the page; other weeks are read by the widget (TanStack Query keys scoped by account, Home and week; invalidated by every chore change).

**API.** `GET/POST/PATCH /api/v1/chore-proposals[/:id]`, `GET/POST /api/v1/chore-occurrences/:id/volunteers`, `POST …/volunteer-correction`, `GET /api/v1/chore-volunteer-requests?homeId`, `PATCH /api/v1/chore-volunteer-requests/:id`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/v1/chore-occurrences/:id/reminder`, `GET /api/v1/chore-schedule?homeId&from&to`, `GET/PUT /api/v1/chore-summary-settings?homeId`. The summary hour's timezone is resolved on the server from the person's settings, never sent by the browser. Same CSRF, JSON, size, session and outcome mapping as S20.

## Consequences

- Two more Trigger.dev tasks (`chore-reminder`, the hourly `chore-digest`); about 720 more short runs a month, well inside the free plan (provider cost register).
- The volunteer race is proved with two real sessions (`chore-volunteers-concurrency.test.ts`); reminders, the move impact, the schedule and the summary in `chore-overview-reminders.test.sql`.
- Points (S23), vacations (S22), wall displays and the main Calendar overlay are not part of S21.
